## Scoring
Calibration is the goal — not just being right, but being *appropriately confident*.

**Brier score:** `(confidence - outcome)²` where outcome = 1 if correct, 0 if wrong. Lower is better. Perfect = 0. Random guessing = 0.25.

## Forecasting Principles
Based on Philip Tetlock's superforecasting research:

1. Think in probabilities, not certainties
2. Update beliefs incrementally when new evidence arrives — don't anchor
3. Use reference classes: what usually happens in situations like this?
4. Seek out disconfirming evidence actively
5. Track your own biases
6. Distinguish between what you *want* to happen and what's *likely* to happen
